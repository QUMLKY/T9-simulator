## Table 7.3, paired data-layer contrasts

Each cell is the mean difference over seeds, its paired 95% confidence interval, and the number of seeds whose difference is positive. A positive difference is not always the improving direction: for the bias-magnitude and log-loss rows the improving direction is negative, so 0/10 there means all ten seeds improved.

On prediction metrics the censoring design forces C4-C3 to equal C2-C1 and C4-C2 to equal C3-C1 exactly. Those rows are kept as an integrity check on the censoring operators. On economic metrics all five pairs differ, because profit comes from the bid rule, which multiplies the two tiers together.
